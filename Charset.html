Our Beginning

The First Tick

In 1987, Elias Laurent opened a tiny workshop on a quiet street in Geneva with nothing more than a workbench, a microscope and an obsession with mechanical precision.

Laurent believed modern watches were becoming increasingly complicated while simultaneously losing their soul.

His answer was Aurelian — a watchmaker devoted to reducing design to its most essential form while obsessing over everything hidden beneath it.

Nearly four decades later, that philosophy remains unchanged.

The Founder

Elias Laurent

"The finest watch is the one you forget is on your wrist."

Born in Lausanne in 1958, Elias Laurent spent his childhood taking apart clocks simply to understand why they moved.

After decades working alongside some of Europe's finest watchmakers, he founded Aurelian in 1987. His philosophy was simple: remove everything unnecessary, perfect everything that remains.
