Our Story
The First Tick

In 1987, Elias Laurent established Aurelian in Geneva with a singular ambition: to create watches that could outlive the moment in which they were created.

The Maison began in a modest workshop overlooking the Rhône. Laurent believed that a watch should reveal nothing at first glance. Its character should emerge slowly, through proportion, movement, and craftsmanship.

That philosophy remains at the heart of Aurelian today.

The Founder
Elias Laurent

Watchmaker. Engineer. Perfectionist.

Laurent believed that mechanical watchmaking was more than engineering. To him, it was architecture in miniature — hundreds of components working together with almost impossible precision.

His guiding principle was simple: make less, but make it better.
